For the original farmhouse, the [Farmhouse](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Farmhouse) upgrades currently cost 10,000g and 450 Wood, 65,000g and 100 Hardwood, and 100,000g, and the second of those costs is the 1.6 price on the [Version History](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Version_History). Each of the three takes three days ([Carpenter's Shop](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Carpenter%27s_Shop)). Order the next one from Robin at the [Carpenter's Shop](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Carpenter%27s_Shop), 24 Mountain Road.

### The second upgrade is 65,000g and 100 Hardwood

In 1.6 the second house upgrade moved from 50,000g and 150 Hardwood to 65,000g and 100 Hardwood ([Version History](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Version_History)). When you buy the second upgrade, bring 65,000g and 100 Hardwood. Before 1.6, that upgrade cost 50,000g and 150 Hardwood ([Version History](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Version_History)).

## Order the upgrade at 24 Mountain Road

Robin is at the Carpenter's Shop, 24 Mountain Road. She sells wood, stone, and furniture, she upgrades the farmhouse, and she constructs farm buildings ([Carpenter's Shop](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Carpenter%27s_Shop)). Go there while the counter is open, ask her to upgrade the farmhouse, and pay the gold and materials for your next tier.

Carry one tier at a time. For the first upgrade, carry 10,000g and 450 Wood when that Wood is already yours. For the second upgrade, carry 65,000g and 100 Hardwood. For the third upgrade, carry 100,000g. The 100 Hardwood is its own stack. It is a separate material from the Wood Robin sells.

On an ordinary week the counter runs from 9:00am to 5:00pm. Tuesday is closed, so a Tuesday visit cannot place the upgrade. Friday closes at 4:00pm, so on Friday the order has to be placed before then. On the other ordinary days, place it between 9:00am and 5:00pm. The house itself can be entered from 9:00am to 8:00pm even while the shop is closed ([Carpenter's Shop](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Carpenter%27s_Shop)). After the counter has closed, and until 8:00pm, the door still opens. The upgrade waits until the counter is open again.

For rain, a festival, the Summer 18 clinic visit, and the times she walks past the counter, use [Robin's shop and outdoor buildings](https://stardewvalleyplanner.art/carpenter-stardew).

Every farmhouse upgrade takes three days ([Farmhouse](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Farmhouse)). The kitchen, the added rooms, or the cellar from that payment is in the house after those three days. Most of her other construction finishes in two days, at the start of the second day after the order ([Carpenter's Shop](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Carpenter%27s_Shop)). That two-day finish is the clock for those other buildings. A farmhouse upgrade stays on three days.

The three days run from the payment until the rooms from that upgrade are finished. On the day you pay, that order's rooms are still unfinished. Marriage can use the first upgrade once the upgrade is in place ([Marriage](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Marriage)). The Mermaid's Pendant needs the first upgrade's construction finished, so the pendant comes after that three-day build ([Mermaid's Pendant](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Mermaid%27s_Pendant)). A renovation, once the second upgrade is finished, is a separate order and finishes immediately.

In multiplayer, a farmhand pays the same three costs, waits the same three days, and can upgrade only their own cabin ([Cabin](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Cabin)). A farmhand cannot upgrade the main farmhouse for another player.

### Buying the 450 Wood changes the gold you bring

Robin sells Wood for 10g in year 1 and for 50g in year 2 or later ([Carpenter's Shop](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Carpenter%27s_Shop)). The first upgrade's 10,000g fee stays 10,000g in both years. The gold you receive for selling Wood does not rise with her year-2 shop price ([Carpenter's Shop](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Carpenter%27s_Shop)).

Use this bill only for the first upgrade, only when Robin sells you all 450 Wood, and only with no other spending added. In year 1, 450 Wood at 10g comes to 4,500g. Add the 10,000g fee and you bring 14,500g ([Farmhouse](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Farmhouse)). That trip buys the 450 Wood, so the payment is the gold total. In year 2 or later, 450 Wood at 50g comes to 22,500g. Add the same 10,000g fee and you bring 32,500g ([Farmhouse](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Farmhouse)).

When the 450 Wood is already in your chests, both years look the same at the counter. Bring 10,000g and the 450 Wood. Her 10g or 50g Wood price stays out of that payment.

## A renovation is not the next upgrade

![Two paths and two examples: Upgrade with 3 days; Renovation with After upgrade 2, Instant, and Refunds gold; Remove crib with Free; Dining room with 150,000g.](/blog/illustrations/house-upgrade-stardew/upgrade-or-renovation.en.webp)

Schematic. The upgrade path reads Upgrade and 3 days. The renovation path reads Renovation, After upgrade 2, Instant, and Refunds gold. Remove crib reads Free. Dining room reads 150,000g.

House renovations unlock after the second farmhouse upgrade ([Carpenter's Shop](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Carpenter%27s_Shop)). You order one and it finishes immediately. Undo it and Robin returns the gold you spent. The refund arrives at once, and the expanded area has to be unoccupied ([Farmhouse](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Farmhouse)).

Remove Crib and Add Dining Room are renovations, separate from the third upgrade. Remove Crib is free and finishes at once ([Carpenter's Shop](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Carpenter%27s_Shop)). Add Dining Room costs 150,000g, adds a room with a footprint of 9×10 plus 4×5, and finishes at once ([Carpenter's Shop](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Carpenter%27s_Shop)). To undo the dining room and get the 150,000g back, that 9×10 plus 4×5 area has to be empty.

The third farmhouse upgrade costs 100,000g, takes three days, and adds the cellar under the kitchen ([Farmhouse](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Farmhouse)). Choose that payment for the cellar, and choose Remove Crib or Add Dining Room when you want those changes instead.
