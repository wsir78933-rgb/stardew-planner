AI Overview
In Stardew Valley, you can upgrade your farmhouse three times by visiting Robin at the Carpenter's Shop. Each upgrade takes 3 days to build. 
Stardew Valley Wiki
 +1
First Upgrade
Cost: 10,000g + 450 Wood
Features: Adds a kitchen with a working counter and fridge, expands the bedroom, upgrades the bed from single to double, and unlocks the ability to get married or invite Krobus to be a roommate. 
Stardew Valley Wiki
Second Upgrade
Cost: 65,000g + 100 Hardwood
Features: Adds two new rooms (one empty room and a nursery with a crib and two child beds to have children), enlarges the kitchen and bedroom, and unlocks house renovations and exterior painting. 
Stardew Valley Wiki
Third Upgrade (Cellar)
Cost: 100,000g
Features: Adds a cellar under the kitchen equipped with 33 casks to age wine and cheese (holding up to 189 casks) to increase their quality and value. 
Stardew Valley Wiki
 +1
House Renovations
