## 3. ReaderTask

### Reader conditions

The reader:

- has found, or is trying to obtain, a Pine Cone and wants a normal Pine rather than a fruit tree;
- may be planting on the farm or checking a natural Pine outside the farm;
- may be asking why a planted seedling is not progressing;
- needs Pine Tar for a verified use or wants to decide whether a mature Pine is worth keeping;
- does not need to be a planner user.

### Reader actions

1. Match **Pine Cone → Pine Tree → Pine Tar** before applying any tree rule.
2. Obtain a Pine Cone through the verified source set and plant it only under the valid, un-tilled-ground and map conditions; do not water it as a crop.
3. If growth stalls at stage 4, inspect all eight adjacent tiles for a mature tree; use a one-tile gap as a practical layout choice, not as a fruit-tree 3×3 rule.
4. Choose unfertilized growth or apply Tree Fertilizer after planting; preserve the seasonal/map exceptions and do not force one disputed median into a countdown.
5. Put a normal or Heavy Tapper on a mature Pine, distinguish the production interval, and know that Pine Tapper production continues in Winter.
6. Use at least one verified Pine Tar use to make a keep/tap decision without turning the page into a cross-tree profit ranking.

### Completion test

The article passes only if a reader can complete the chain without opening a second guide:

- name the Pine Cone, Pine Tree, and Pine Tar relationship correctly;
- plant a Pine Cone under the valid-ground and map limits without watering it;
- diagnose a stage-4 stall by checking all eight neighboring tiles and avoid importing a fruit-tree 3×3 rule;
- distinguish normal growth conditions from Tree Fertilizer behavior and understand why no single “Pine takes X days” countdown is promised;
- attach a Tapper only to a mature Pine and distinguish normal **5 nights** from Heavy Tapper **2 days**, including Winter production;
- name at least one verified Pine Tar use and understand that the page does not establish the most profitable tree;
- if the planner CTA is present, understand it as layout sketching only, not a growth, Tapper, or Pine Tar simulator.

### Scope boundary

**Include:** Pine identity; Pine Cone sources needed to begin; valid planting and no-watering conditions; common-tree adjacency diagnosis; practical one-tile spacing; normal growth and Tree Fertilizer conditions; Winter and map exceptions that change the decision; normal/Heavy Tapper timing; a compact verified Pine Tar use check; natural Pine interaction limits needed by the task; one optional planner CTA.

**Do not include:** a cross-tree profit table; a full all-trees encyclopedia; Maple/Oak Green Rain or Fall behavior; an exact Pine growth median or countdown; a numeric in-game Pine footprint; fruit-tree 3×3 orchard rules; a full planner tutorial or unverified planner screenshot; an exhaustive Pine Cone recipe/trading guide; a Pine Tar standalone price/profit article; lore, mods, or a “fir tree” identification page.

The boundary is editorial control. Do not write “this article does not cover …” in the body; leave secondary needs to a natural internal link or omit them.
