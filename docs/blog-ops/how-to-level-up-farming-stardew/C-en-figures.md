| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Type | Accurate schematic. Controllable drawing. Not a fake screenshot of a harvest popup. |
| Reader must see | On one plant, the first product of a harvest counts for Farming XP; extras on that same harvest do not. |
| Layout | Three one-harvest rows, same rule: **Blueberry**, **Cranberry**, **Potato**. Left: first item with a clear XP mark. Right: extra berries or extra tuber with a clear “no Farming XP” mark. Optional small note under blueberry and cranberry: “Next harvest date still grants XP.” |
| Numbers on the art | Blueberry first product **10**. Cranberry first product **14**. Potato first product **14**. Extra items **0** Farming XP. |
| Caption already in draft | “First product only, on one harvest. A blueberry pull that yields three berries still grants 10 Farming XP once. A cranberry pull that yields two berries still grants 14 once. A potato pull that yields an extra tuber still grants 14 once. The next harvest date on a blueberry or cranberry plant is a new harvest, and that new harvest grants XP again. This is the Farming page rule, not a count of berries in a test save.” |
| Alt already in draft | “One blueberry, cranberry, or potato harvest: the first product is marked as Farming XP, the extra berries or extra tuber are marked as no Farming XP. Not a game screenshot.” |
| Facts | K3, K16 (potato 14), K34 (blueberry 10), K49 (cranberry 14) |
| Quality | Do not tint extras gold/iridium as if quality changed XP. K7: quality does not change harvest XP. If quality stars appear at all, keep the XP number identical on normal and gold. |
| Forbidden on the art | XP per day; gold/day cells from the seasonal articles; Winter Seeds; a second panel that pretends to be in-game UI. |

Berry counts in the caption (three blueberries, two cranberries) are the usual harvest picture for teaching the first-product rule. They are not a claim that every harvest always drops that many extras. The XP cells 10 / 14 / 14 are the wiki table. Extra-count artwork is illustrative of “multiples,” which the Farming page names for these three crops.
