### Section 1: How to Harvest Standard Crops by Hand
- **Core Instructions**:
  - Explain that standard crops (such as Parsnips) are harvested directly by hand through interaction rather than swinging tools.
  - State the positive PC control: stand adjacent to or hover over the mature crop and press **Right-Click** or the **X** key.
  - Explain that Right-Click / X maps to the game's **Check / Do Action** command. Note that Left-Click / C is defined in Controls as **Use tool or place item** (used for planting seeds, placing fertilizer, and using tools).
  - Detail the continuous hold-and-walk technique: players can hold down Right-Click (or hold the X key) and walk through crop rows to harvest ripe crops automatically in their path.
- **Strict Scope Boundaries**:
  - Do NOT claim that Left-Click is forbidden or that Left-Click cannot harvest.
  - Do NOT claim that players must have empty hands or must unequip all tools.
  - Do NOT invent crop harvest buttons for console or mobile platforms. (General platform button definitions from Controls oldid 192481 may be referenced as platform context, but do not state that gamepad A/Cross or mobile tap is a verified crop button).

### Section 2: Crops That Require a Scythe to Harvest
- **Core Instructions**:
  - Identify the specific crops documented as requiring a scythe swing:
    - **Kale** (Spring, 6 days)
    - **Amaranth** (Fall, 7 days)
    - **Wheat** (Summer & Fall, 4 days; also has a 40% chance to drop hay when harvested with any scythe)
    - **Unmilled Rice** (Spring, 8 days on dry land; 6 days if irrigated within 3 tiles of water)
    - **Fiber Seeds** (All seasons including Winter, 7 days, requires no watering)
  - Compare the three scythe variants based on documented facts:
    - **Starter Scythe**: Present in inventory from day 1; consumes 0 energy; cuts weeds, grass, and moss; harvests scythe crops; cannot be upgraded at the Blacksmith; cannot be lost on death (1.6+).
    - **Golden Scythe**: Found at the Grim Reaper statue in the Quarry Mine; has a larger swing radius, higher chance to cut grass, 75% hay from grass, and 13 damage. State accurately that its documented benefits do not include harvesting regular crops.
    - **Iridium Scythe**: Obtained from Farming Mastery in the Mastery Cave (1.6+); explicitly harvests any crop grown on tilled soil within its swing radius (including standard hand-picked crops, Garden Pots, and forage from Wild Seeds).
- **Strict Scope Boundaries**:
  - Do NOT claim that basic or Golden Scythes destroy living regular crops (Parsnips).
  - Do NOT claim that Golden Scythes "only" cut scythe crops or "cannot" touch regular crops; state neutrally that harvesting regular crops is not among its listed benefits, whereas the Iridium Scythe explicitly introduces that capability.
  - Do NOT claim the scythe-crop list is exhaustive.
  - Do NOT pad with grass hay mechanics, Silo guides, or Farming Mastery leveling costs.

### Section 3: How to Tell When Your Crops Are Ready to Harvest
- **Core Instructions**:
  - Explain how growth days work: seed packet growth times exclude the day the seed was planted ($N$-day crop planted and watered on Day 1 matures on Day $1 + N$; e.g., 4-day Parsnip matures on Day 5).
  - Note the watering requirement: growth advances only when watered; unwatered days freeze growth and delay harvest.
  - Distinguish single-harvest crops from regrowing crops using only verified examples:
    - **Single-Harvest**: Harvesting collects the produce and removes the plant, leaving empty tilled soil (e.g., Parsnip, Cauliflower, Melon, Pumpkin).
    - **Regrowing**: Harvesting picks the mature yield while keeping the plant alive to produce recurring harvests (e.g., Green Bean regrows every 3 days on a trellis; Strawberry regrows every 4 days; Coffee Bean continues producing after maturity).
- **Strict Scope Boundaries**:
  - Do NOT cite unverified crop examples (e.g., do not cite Blueberries, Cranberries, or Potatoes).
  - Do NOT state a specific regrowth day count for Coffee Bean (only Green Bean 3 days and Strawberry 4 days are verified).
  - Do NOT mention a "green plus" cursor (not supported by wiki evidence).
- **Verified Sources & Citations**:

### Section 4: What Happens If You Leave Crops Unharvested?
- **Core Instructions**:
  - Explain within-season survival: mature crops left unpicked outdoors remain in the ground overnight within their active season without withering.
  - Explain season rollover: when the season rolls over from Day 28 to Day 1 of the next month, outdoor single-season crops wither and die, leaving dead plants that can be cleared with a scythe.
  - Document the verified exceptions:
    1. **Multi-season crops**: Crops whose growth span covers consecutive seasons (Ancient Fruit, Coffee Bean, Corn, Sunflower, Wheat) do not die at the transition between their valid seasons. Fully grown Wheat left on Summer 28 survives to Fall 1, preserving tilled soil and fertilizer.
    2. **The Greenhouse**: Crops grown inside a repaired Greenhouse never die from seasonal transitions and grow year-round.
- **Strict Scope Boundaries**:
  - Do NOT claim that mature crops are immune to crows.
  - Do NOT make absolute assertions that crops "never spoil or rot under any circumstance".
  - Do NOT claim that a full player inventory drops crops to the ground (that rule applies only to Junimo Huts).
- **Verified Sources & Citations**:
  - Stardew Valley Wiki Crops: [Crops](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Crops) (oldid 193979)
  - Stardew Valley Wiki Greenhouse: [Greenhouse](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Greenhouse) (oldid 194164)

### Section 5: Special Harvesting Rules: Giant Crops and Trellises
- **Core Instructions**:
  - **Giant Crops (3×3 Grid)**:
    - Fully grown Cauliflower (Spring), Melon (Summer), Pumpkin (Fall), Powdermelon (Winter 1.6+), and Qi Fruit in a 3×3 grid have a chance to combine into a giant crop when the top-left tile is fully grown and watered.
    - Giant crops cannot be harvested by hand or with a scythe; they must be chopped with an Axe (taking 3 hits).
    - Drops 15 to 21 normal crops. Giant crops do not die at season rollover.
  - **Navigating Impassable Trellis Crops**:
    - Trellis crops (Green Bean in Spring, Hops in Summer, Grape in Fall) grow on physical trellises that block player movement until the plant is dead.
    - Field planning rule: Players must lay out fields with accessible walking corridors so every trellis tile can be reached by hand.
    - Naturally introduce the [Stardew Valley Farm Planner](https://stardewvalleyplanner.art) as a tool for mapping walkways around trellis rows and sprinkler ranges.
- **Strict Scope Boundaries**:
  - Do NOT include automated Junimo Hut harvesting or building instructions in this section.
  - Do NOT use outdated 2021 numbers for giant crops (e.g. center crop, 17–22 items).
