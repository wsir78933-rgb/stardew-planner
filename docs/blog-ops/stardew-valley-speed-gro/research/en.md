## Executive research finding

The original query is underspecified but commercially/content-wise coherent: the visible result set combines an item/mechanics lookup with a decision question about when the fertilizer is worth using. The result family is not limited to “what is Speed-Gro”; it repeatedly exposes four adjacent jobs: obtain/craft it, understand the three fertilizer tiers, decide whether saved time creates a useful extra harvest, and calculate timing/profit for a crop and planting date.

The strongest information gap is a version-aware, evidence-bound decision explanation that connects the official 1.6 recipe change to the current item data and then to crop-calendar timing. Existing pages tend to split these jobs: item pages give mechanics and acquisition, ROI pages give recommendations or simplified formulas, calculators give inputs and outputs, and community discussions expose the rounding/regrowth confusion. This is an opportunity signal only; the primary intent remains for the next role to select.

## Verified facts that may be shared with later roles

The following are source-bound facts, not a draft. Source class and limits are recorded in [source-facts.md](en-evidence/source-facts.md).

- **F1, first-party changelog:** Stardew Valley’s official 1.6 changelog says Speed-Gro now requires 5 Moss instead of 1 Clam and Deluxe Speed-Gro now requires 5 bone fragments instead of 1 coral. This is the cleanest authority for the version-change warning.
- **F2, current community item page:** The current Speed-Gro page states at least a 10% growth-rate increase; Farming Level 3; Pine Tar (1) plus Moss (5); five units per craft; Pierre’s, Crafting, or Bone Mill as sources; Pierre’s price 100g from Spring 15 of Year 1; and a 20-unit Spring Crops Bundle reward.
- **F3, application boundary:** The Speed-Gro page states that it can be placed before or after planting or at any crop-growth stage, and does not reduce the time between harvests for multi-harvest crops. The Fertilizer page describes the same first-harvest boundary in its mechanics section.
- **F4, tier facts:** Current item pages state at least 25% for Deluxe and at least 33% for Hyper; the pages provide their recipe/unlock and acquisition details. The official changelog separately confirms the 1.6 Deluxe ingredient change.
- **F5, calendar evidence:** The Crop Growth Calendars page provides Base, Speed-Gro, Deluxe Speed-Gro, Hyper Speed-Gro, and Agriculturist comparison tables across crops. It explicitly says the 10% table also applies to Agriculturist and describes its replanting assumption for non-multi-harvest crops.

No claim that a specific crop is “best,” that an ROI is positive, or that a specific number of extra harvests is guaranteed is promoted to a shared fact here. Such claims require a crop-calendar calculation with stated planting date, season, version assumptions, and source support.

## Research conclusions and boundaries

- The spelling/spacing variant resolves to the hyphenated item name in the live result set. Preserve the user’s query wording for research intent, but use the canonical item name only when the evidence supports it.
- A useful new angle is the “advertised percentage versus season-calendar result” distinction. This addresses the visible Reddit/Steam confusion without copying their unsourced calculations.
- A second angle is “current 1.6 recipe plus tier comparison.” The official changelog supplies a first-party correction that older Fandom snippets and community guides may not reflect.
- A third angle is a transparent crop-plan workflow: choose crop and planting day, read the current calendar, account for fertilizer cost and regrow boundary, then decide. This can support a tool-oriented page without claiming a universal best crop.
- No live game run, save-file inspection, code execution against game data, or real calculator-output verification was performed. Any later example must be labelled as a calculation/example and independently checked.
- Search ranking, result order, AI summaries, competitor claims, and community comments are internal research evidence only; they must not appear as reader-facing proof or “ranked first” language.
