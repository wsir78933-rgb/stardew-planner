# Opened source facts and evidence boundaries

All pages in this file were opened directly in the local Ego-browser on 2026-09-26. Search snippets were not used as proof of the facts below. A fact is marked **shareable** only when the opened page itself supports it. Community-wiki facts remain community-source facts; they are not relabelled as first-party game documentation.

## F2 — current regular Speed-Gro item data

- **Type:** topic knowledge; community-maintained game-data page. The official Stardew Valley site’s navigation links to the same wiki, but that does not turn every wiki statement into first-party documentation.
- **Source:** [Speed-Gro - Stardew Valley Wiki](https://www.stardewvalleywiki.com/Speed-Gro)
- **Observed page revision link:** `https://stardewvalleywiki.com/mediawiki/index.php?title=Speed-Gro&oldid=190630`
- **Observed support:** the page states at least a 10% growth-rate increase; source `Pierre's • Crafting • Bone Mill`; Farming Level 3; Pine Tar (1) plus Moss (5); five Speed-Gro per craft; Pierre’s 100g price from Spring 15 of Year 1; 20 units from the Spring Crops Bundle; before/after planting or any crop-growth stage; no reduction to the time between harvests for multi-harvest crops.
- **Shareable:** yes, with the current page URL and a version/revision note if precision matters.
- **Limit:** this is not an in-game test by this role. The page’s game-code references were observed as citations on the page, not independently executed.

## F3 — late application and multi-harvest boundary

- **Type:** topic knowledge; community-maintained game-data page.
- **Source:** [Speed-Gro - Stardew Valley Wiki](https://www.stardewvalleywiki.com/Speed-Gro) and [Fertilizer - Stardew Valley Wiki](https://www.stardewvalleywiki.com/Fertilizer)
- **Observed support:** the Speed-Gro page says applying after planting still decreases time to fully grow, while stage reductions already passed do not retroactively apply. It also says the fertilizer does not reduce the interval between multi-harvests. The Fertilizer page’s mechanics section says Speed-Gro affects the growth and first harvest of a multi-harvest crop.
- **Shareable:** yes, with careful wording: “initial growth/first harvest” rather than “all harvests.”
- **Limit:** crop-by-crop outcomes still require the calendar tables and planting assumptions; do not infer a universal number of days saved.
